# Community conduct

We want Kusanya to be welcoming to contributors and the people who use it. Treat
others with respect, welcome different experience levels, discuss ideas on their
merits and make criticism specific and constructive.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and disclosure of
private credentials or personal information are not acceptable in project spaces,
including issues, pull requests and discussions.

Report conduct concerns privately to Cobitech Solutions at
`cobitechsolutions@gmail.com`. Share only the information needed to investigate;
do not post private reports in public issues. Maintainers will treat reports
confidentially as far as practical, consider the affected person's safety and
respond proportionately. Actions can include removal of content, a warning,
temporary restrictions or a ban. Maintainers are held to the same standard.

Good-faith reports and participation must not lead to retaliation.
